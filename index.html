How to Play

  • Allen is weak. He only throws stones. Your summons are your army.
  • Every card has a special skill: hold the skill key and press its slot key (touch: long-press the card).
  • Each summon takes grimoire capacity. Leveling raises capacity, HP and mana.
  • New summon ranks unlock as you level: Insect → Beast → Bird → Grass → Stone → Fish → Spirit → Dragon.
  • Kills fill the Soul gauge. When full, choose a boost for the rest of the stage.
  • Spend ◆ magic stones in the Grimoire: enhance cards, fuse two Lv 5 cards into a new creature, and buy talents.
  • Each stage has 2 optional challenges for bonus stars. Clear Stage 3 to open the endless Abyss Tower.
